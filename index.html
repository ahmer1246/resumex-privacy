ResumeX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

ResumeX (“the app”) is a resume builder and ATS checker for Android, developed by Muhammad Ahmed (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information you enter in the app

The details you type into ResumeX (name, email, phone number, location, links, photo, work experience, education, projects, skills, languages, job descriptions and cover letter details) are stored only on your device. We do not have accounts, we do not collect this information, and we do not upload it to any server.

2. Uploaded CV files

When you choose a CV file (PDF, DOCX or TXT) for the ATS check, the app reads the text from the file on your device. The file and its text are not sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Photos and files

  • If you add a profile photo, the app uses the system photo picker. The photo is saved inside the app’s data on your device.
  • When you export a PDF, it is created on your device. It is saved or shared only where you choose (for example your Files app, WhatsApp, email or Google Drive). Any app you share it with handles it under its own privacy policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ResumeX shows ads using Google AdMob (banner and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect and use certain data, such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show ads and measure their performance. This data is handled by Google, not by us. You can learn more and manage ad settings here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How AdMob uses data

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Coins and app settings

Your coin balance, unlocked templates and design choices are saved locally on your device so the app can remember them. They are not sent to any server.

6. Data we share

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share your resume data with anyone, because we never receive it. The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Because we do not store your data, there is nothing to delete on our side.

8. Security

Your resume data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. Please keep your phone secure and be careful when sharing exported PDFs, as they contain the personal details you entered.

9. Children’s privacy

ResumeX is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the “Last updated” date changed.
